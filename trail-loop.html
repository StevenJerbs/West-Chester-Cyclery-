CASCADE LOOP

A level-sized trail loop: 1.8 km, an 837 m descent at −9.2% through eleven zones, closed by a fire-road climb. Large rollers, a five-table jump line, two rock drops, a road gap, two rock gardens and seventeen berms of narrow singletrack — ridden on a Starling Murmur (steel single pivot, 140 / 150 mm, the bike the mtbkin keypoint model tracks most tightly) with the rider model and physics of the Suspension Lab, on a course whose geometry and rideability are both gated by tests before it ships. Sunlight and ambient occlusion are path-traced in Blender and baked in; the browser never traces a ray.
