How to read it: the dashed line on each travel bar is static sag; the amber arrow is dynamic ride height (a rolling average of travel used). Add rebound clicks and the damper returns slower than the whoops arrive — the arrow sinks and the suspension packs down, riding deep with little travel left. Back the rebound out to 0–2 clicks and the return velocity spikes: watch the bike pogo off the DROPS landing. Compression clicks slow the stroke into the bump instead; spring rate sets where sag sits.
