PUMP RUN

A side-scroller on the Pump Lab's physics: the speed comes from your legs against the trough load, the crest, the lip and the landing. Same rider, five bikes, one line down the valley.

PICK A BIKE

TILT THE PHONE
Roll it like the bars: right end down puts your weight over the front, left end down sends it back. Keys: ← →
RIGHT THUMB · PEDAL
Hold to pedal. Stamina runs out; coast to get it back. Key: ↑ or W
LEFT THUMB · BRAKE
Hold to brake. Brake hard with your weight forward on a steep and you go over the bars. Key: ↓ or S

Pumping: come up tall through the bottom of the trough, press forward over the crest, pull back and up off a lip, weight back off a drop, level the bike to the landing. Cased landings cost speed; a bad one costs the run.
